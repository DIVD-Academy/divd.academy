Partners

Partners

DIVD Academy works with schools, mentors and organizations who share our goal of making cybersecurity education accessible to young talent. Partnerships take different forms, depending on what each partner is best placed to offer.

What a partnership can look like

Schools and education partners

Organizations that help introduce students to cybersecurity, or that recognize Academy learning as part of a broader curriculum.

Mentors

Experienced professionals who volunteer their time to guide trainees through practical work and traineeships.

Organizations

Businesses and institutions that support the Academy's mission, whether by offering resources, expertise or practical opportunities through DIVD.Works.

Interested in partnering with us?

If your school, organization or team would like to explore ways to support DIVD Academy learners, we'd like to hear from you.
